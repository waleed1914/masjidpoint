Questions

Things people ask

Who decides which businesses appear?

The masjid does. A business applies to advertise through a specific masjid, and nothing goes live until that masjid approves it and the payment is verified.

How much does a masjid keep?

Each masjid sets its own monthly advertising price, job fee and split. The share is agreed up front, recorded against every invoice, and settled back to the masjid.

Is the price fixed once I apply?

Yes. The rate is captured when you apply, so a later change to a masjid's pricing never re-prices an application already in progress.

How does the masjid shop work?

Order online, then collect from the masjid or have it delivered where offered. You can pay by bank transfer up front or pay the masjid when you collect.

What does it cost to register a masjid?

Nothing. A masjid registers free, sets its own rates, and only ever receives money through the platform.
